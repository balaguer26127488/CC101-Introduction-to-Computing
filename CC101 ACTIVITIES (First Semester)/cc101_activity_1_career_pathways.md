## III. Background

Computing is not just "programming." As introduced in the Unit 2 lecture, the field branches into several distinct career pathways, each with its own operational scope, required technical competencies, representative job titles, and professional standard or reference body: **Software Engineering**, **Systems & Cloud Infrastructure**, **Data Science & AI Engineering**, **Cybersecurity & Threat Defense**, and **Information Systems & Enterprise IT**. Each pathway also has its own career roadmap—the entry-level, mid-level, senior-level, and expert/leadership roles a professional typically grows through. This activity asks you to review those five pathways, choose the one that interests you most, and map out what growing in that pathway could look like for you.

## VI. Realization (Reflection)

1. **Why did you choose this particular pathway over the other four?**  
   I selected Software Engineering because it provides a direct outlet for logic and creativity by allowing me to build software solutions from the ground up. While areas like cloud infrastructure and cybersecurity are essential, building user-facing applications and solving complex algorithmic problems aligns best with my personal interests.

2. **Looking at your roadmap, which career stage excites you the most, and why?**  
   The Senior-Level stage excites me the most because it strikes a balance between technical expertise and leadership. At this stage, I can design major system architectures, influence project direction, and mentor junior engineers while staying deeply involved in hands-on development.

3. **Which required skill or competency for this pathway do you already have, and which one will be hardest for you to develop?**  
   I already have a working knowledge of Object-Oriented Programming principles and fundamental code structures. The most challenging competency to develop will likely be mastering scalable system design and high-volume database performance, as these require extensive real-world experience.

4. **How does mapping this roadmap change or confirm your plans for the rest of this course and your degree?**  
   Mapping this roadmap confirms that I am on the right academic path while giving me a clearer focus for my elective choices. It highlights the need to build practical project portfolios early and focus heavily on database management and automated testing alongside my core coursework.
